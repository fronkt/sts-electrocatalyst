**S27700.** The accessible [Nature Communications article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-67439-4) names the computed phase as γ-NiFeOOH and describes an oxygen vacancy near Fe. It explicitly assigns theoretical OER overpotentials of 1.14 eV (pristine NiFeOOH, Fe site), 1.36 eV (pristine NiFeOOH, Ni site), 0.19 eV (NiFeOOH-Vo, Fe site), and 0.68 eV (NiFeOOH-Vo, Ni site). Its Fig. 6g caption says the chlorine-water Pourbaix diagram is “based on the corresponding theoretical overpotential” of NiFeOOH-Vo and IrO2. This is the same article already reviewed locally; it does not specify the plotted models' crystallographic facet/termination or label each Fig. 6g potential line as eta or minimum UL. The article cites the authors' [Figshare Source Data DOI](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.30704777). Root's `phase/source_data_dedup.json` receipt establishes that Figshare deposit 30704777.v1 file 59826623 (`source data.xlsx`, 225118706 bytes, MD5 `5810e9b6a9988ae8e628dd5aea4f2282`) exactly matches the cached `files_si/S27700_SI1.xlsx`; this is duplicate source data, not a new-evidence lead. A Research Square preprint was also surfaced but repeats the 0.19 eV claim and provides no distinct facet assignment in the indexed material. Hold retained.
