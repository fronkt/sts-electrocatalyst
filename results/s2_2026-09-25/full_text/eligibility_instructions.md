# Full-text eligibility — instructions v5 (2026-09-27; identical for every read)

v5 applies the entrant's v5 rulings of 2026-09-27 (`reconcile/entrant_decisions.md`): the answers to the fourteen v4-triage questions (D1–D14, `reconcile/v4_questions_triage.md`), adopted with the entrant's explicit conditions on D4 and D10. It builds on v4. v4 is kept as `eligibility_instructions_v4.md` and v3 as `eligibility_instructions_v3.md`; their decisions stay on record for the sensitivity comparison. Changes from v4 are marked **[v5 Dn]**.

You decide whether each paper belongs to a registered review population by reading its full text. You decide **eligibility only**.

## Population

The population is primary research articles first published **2011-01-01 through 2026-09-18** that **calculate the oxygen evolution reaction (OER) on a rutile-oxide (110) surface** and **report a computational-hydrogen-electrode (CHE) overpotential** in the article or its supporting information.

- Comparative or multi-material computational studies qualify whatever the authors call them.
- Reviews, perspectives and purely experimental work do not qualify.
- Studies with no rutile (110) calculation do not qualify.
- Studies that do not report a CHE overpotential do not qualify.
- **[v5 D9] One model decides.** A paper qualifies through any one computed model for which E3–E6 are all `YES`. Judge E3–E6 one model at a time and never combine evidence from different models (for example E4 from a benchmark with E5 or E6 from a new phase). A relative η that compares this model with other surfaces still belongs to this model. Before answering `NO` on E3–E6, check every computed model in the paper, including benchmark or comparison models (for example a rutile IrO2 benchmark next to a new phase). Report E3–E6 for one model: a model with all `YES` if there is one; otherwise a model with no `NO`; otherwise the model whose first `NO` comes latest. When the paper has more than one model, name the reported model in `note`.

## Criteria

Judge each criterion from the paper itself (the main text and any SI that is in your file). For each, give a verdict of `YES`, `NO` or `UNCLEAR`, plus the location (page, section or figure) and a short verbatim excerpt of 40 words or fewer that supports it. **[v5 D1]** When a verdict needs two passages (for example phase evidence and its connection to the model), give both in `excerpt`, joined by " … ", each 25 words or fewer, and both locations in `where`.

- **E1 — primary research article.**
  - Not a review, perspective, editorial, erratum, peer-review report or book.
  - Not a conference abstract or thesis. For those, note the form and whether the text names a journal article that carries the work.
  - A genuine review or perspective is `NO` even when it contains new calculations. Judge the form from both the journal's label and the content; when they disagree, say so in the note.
  - **[v5 D7]** A complete primary-research preprint counts as a primary research article (`form` `"preprint"`). A standalone technical report that is not itself an article manuscript is `NO`, like abstract-only records and theses, even when it is tied to an article; as for those, note the form and whether the text names a journal article or preprint that carries the work. A report or repository wrapper that contains an identifiable article manuscript is judged as that manuscript.
  - **[v5 D7]** A linked journal version is handled by version linking, outside this read. There, a journal version first published after 2026-09-18 does not replace the in-window preprint: the preprint is judged on its own text and posting date, and both versions keep their dates on record. If the preprint names its journal version, give the venue and date in `note`.
  - **[v5 D11]** Reusing published data alone (no new calculation in this study) is `UNCLEAR` for E1. Say so in the note; E3 then decides the case. Set `secondary` and `provenance` only as the E3 re-analysis bullet says; mere quotation or replotting leaves both null.
- **E2 — first publication date 2011-01-01 to 2026-09-18.** Use the first-publication (online or print) date printed on the paper. Received and accepted dates, and a year inside the DOI, are not publication dates. If no publication date is printed, answer `UNCLEAR`; dates are then settled from publisher and Crossref records outside this read.
  - **[v5 D7]** For a preprint, use the posting date of its first public version (version 1), not an author-typed date in the manuscript. Name the version whose date you quote. If the file shows only a later version's date and it falls after 2026-09-18, E2 is `UNCLEAR`, not `NO`. Preprint posting dates are verified from the server record outside this read.
- **E3 — the authors themselves calculate OER in this study.** This means they compute the OER intermediates (*OH, *O, *OOH or equivalent) or the steps with DFT or a similar electronic-structure method.
  - Quoting or plotting numbers from other work is not enough. A citation next to a value does not settle who computed it: check the methods, captions and SI text in your file.
  - Declared approximations are allowed, for example *OOH from a stated scaling relation (ΔG_OOH = ΔG_OH + 3.2 eV) or cycle closure.
  - **[v5 D3]** One newly computed intermediate, adsorption energy or PCET step is enough for E3 when the authors explicitly use it to investigate the oxidation of water or hydroxide to O2. A full cycle is not required for E3; E6 still needs a reported CHE result.
  - **[v5 D3]** Species identity is not reaction identity. Calculations made only for another reaction (chlorine evolution, CO2 reduction, HER, NRR, Li–O2 chemistry), for photo-generated holes, or as generic adsorption benchmarks are `NO`, even when they contain O, OH or OOH. OER analysed as a competing reaction counts. A photo-generated species counts only when it is studied explicitly as an intermediate of O2 evolution.
  - **[v5 D3]** Machine-learned potentials and benchmark datasets: reused dataset entries and pretrained-model predictions are not new calculations. A newly computed electronic-structure subset counts only when the paper uses it to investigate OER, not only to report a prediction error. A pre-existing structure used only to plot a PDOS is not a new OER calculation.
  - **[v5 D2, D3]** Computing only part of the cycle (a surface Pourbaix diagram of *OH/*O, one PCET step, intermediates for spectra) satisfies E3 only when the authors explicitly study those species or that step as part of the oxidation of water or hydroxide to O2; this holds even when the immediate analysis is stability or spectra. A surface Pourbaix or spectroscopy study with no stated connection to OER does not get E3 `YES` from oxygen-containing species alone. A partial cycle does not by itself satisfy E6. A two-electron peroxide pathway is not O2 evolution.
  - **[v5 D11]** Re-analysis of existing electronic-structure data with no new qualifying calculation is `NO` for the primary population, whether the data are the authors' own, another group's or mixed. Set `secondary` to `"reanalysis"` only for such an identifiable original re-analysis. Then set `provenance`: `"own"` or `"external"` when the paper shows whose data they are, `"mixed"` when it shows both, and `"unclear"` when the data are clearly reused but their owner is not shown. One shared coauthor alone does not show ownership. Otherwise both fields are null. Mere quotation or replotting is not original re-analysis. If it is unclear whether any calculation is new, E3 is `UNCLEAR`. A paper with reused inputs and also new qualifying OER calculations can pass.
- **E4 — the surface is a rutile-structured oxide.** Examples: RuO2, IrO2, TiO2 (rutile), SnO2, MnO2 (β/rutile), PbO2, VO2(R), CrO2, doped or mixed rutile phases, and "rutile-type" solid solutions.
  - The rutile polymorph must be stated or plainly established **for the computed model**.
  - Plainly established: a phase-specific diffraction card identified with rutile (e.g. RuO2 PDF/JCPDS 43-1027, IrO2 15-0870, SnO2 41-1445, TiO2 rutile 21-1276), an explicitly rutile structure reference or CIF, or a stated unit cell and atomic arrangement that is rutile. Quote the evidence.
  - **[v5 D1]** Rutile identified on the synthesized material (a diffraction card or indexed XRD, Raman, EXAFS or HRTEM result, a space group) counts for the model when the paper documents the connection: the model is presented as a model of that characterized catalyst, a caption or methods sentence connects the structure to the model, or the doped model's parent is the identified rutile phase; and no contradictory phase information appears. No set wording is required: a sentence such as "to understand the activity of the synthesized RuO2 nanosheets, we modelled RuO2(110)" is a connection. A shared formula alone is not: a DFT section that models RuO2(110) but never refers to the characterized sample does not count. The reported result must actually identify rutile; naming a technique is not enough. Never carry phase evidence to a support, another component, another polymorph or an unrelated benchmark; a benchmark model takes the evidence only when the paper documents that it represents the characterized material. If the only connection is your own assumption that the sample and the model share a polymorph, E4 is `UNCLEAR`. Quote both the phase evidence and the connection.
  - **[v5 D6]** Judge what a sentence refers to, not where it sits. An introduction or methods sentence that identifies the material actually modelled as rutile counts when it is clearly connected to the model and nothing contradicts it; a defined family of rutile models covers its named members. Generic remarks ("other rutile oxides"), a reference-list title on its own, or background on another study do not. A cited structure counts when the authors explicitly state that they use it for the computed model and the paper identifies that structure as rutile (in the text or in the cited title), and nothing contradicts it. Do not look the reference up or rely on memory of it. If the link to the model stays ambiguous, E4 is `UNCLEAR`.
  - **[v5 D9]** A phase name that denotes the rutile-type structure (space group P4₂/mnm) establishes E4 when the paper applies it to the computed model; it does not by itself establish E5. These names need no further check: rutile TiO2, β-MnO2 (pyrolusite), β-PbO2 (plattnerite), SnO2 (cassiterite), VO2(R), CrO2, and RuO2 or IrO2 named as rutile-type. Record the name in the excerpt and its structure in `note` (e.g. "β-PbO2 = rutile type, P4₂/mnm"). For any other phase name, card or database entry that the paper does not itself call rutile, quote it, answer E4 `UNCLEAR` and write "identity check: <item>" in `note`; the identity is checked at reconciliation, not from memory.
  - **[v5 D9]** "Structurally similar to rutile" is not enough. A distorted-rutile phase of another crystal system (e.g. monoclinic WO2) is not renamed rutile, and a Miller-index resemblance does not make its facet (110). Admitting it would need an explicit scope extension, which v5 does not make. For such a model E4 is `UNCLEAR`, not `NO` (the "other polymorphs" rule below does not apply to it); write "scope extension needed" in `note`, and quote any structural and facet mapping the paper gives. E5 is `NO` only when the paper shows that the modelled facet does not correspond to rutile (110); a different Miller index in the distorted cell does not show this, and without a mapping E5 is `UNCLEAR`.
  - **[v5 D9]** A thin overlayer counts when it demonstrably keeps a rutile (110)-derived surface and the OER and η are assigned to it; "rutile-like" alone is `UNCLEAR`. Being an overlayer rather than a freestanding slab is not a reason for `NO`.
  - Not enough on its own: "tetragonal", the formula ("RuO2"), "RuO2(110)"/"IrO2(110)", or generic CUS/bridge labels. Then E4 is `UNCLEAR`.
  - The evidence must belong to the computed model, not only to another component of the experimental sample.
  - Anatase, perovskite, spinel, amorphous or other polymorphs mean `NO`.
- **E5 — OER is calculated on a (110) surface.** The OER intermediates or steps (E3) are computed on a rutile (110) surface, alone or with other facets. Doped or mixed rutile (110) models count.
  - A periodic slab is not required. A nanoparticle facet, cluster or interface counts only when the paper shows that the computed OER site represents rutile (110). An oxide–oxyhydroxide site is `NO`. "Commercial IrO2" with no computed model is `NO`.
  - **[v5 D8]** "Shows" means a specific statement that the cluster was cut from, built to represent or mapped onto rutile (110), with an identifiable model and site, or a mapping figure. Exact bulk coordination after relaxation is not required, and you need not reconstruct the mapping yourself. A single atom anchored in a bridging-oxygen vacancy of rutile (110), where the parent surface persists and the OER site belongs to that integrated model, is an atom-modified rutile (110) surface and can qualify. A bare label ("RuO2 cluster") or a rutile support is not enough for `YES`. An OER site on a separate particle or cluster that merely sits on or near rutile (for example a metal particle or RuOx cluster on a rutile support), with no statement or figure mapping it to rutile (110), is `NO`; the support's facet does not count. A cluster whose described model is not cut from, built to represent or mapped onto rutile (110) is `NO`. If the paper gives only a label and does not describe the model or how its site relates to rutile (110), E5 is `UNCLEAR`.
  - **[v5 D2, replaces the v4 "other purposes" bullet]** A (110) calculation that involves no qualifying OER intermediates or steps does not satisfy E5: for example electronic structure or PDOS of a bare slab while the OER is computed on another facet, or adsorption of other species. Stability or spectra analyses of species studied as OER intermediates (E3) on rutile (110) do satisfy E5; E6 then decides.
  - If neither the main text nor any SI in your file states which facet the OER was computed on, answer `UNCLEAR`.
- **E6 — a CHE overpotential is reported for the qualifying rutile (110) model.** SI counts equally.
  - A numerical theoretical or limiting overpotential (η) from the free-energy steps counts.
  - **[v5 D12]** A surface-specific CHE quantity that the authors themselves label "overpotential" counts even when it appears to omit the 1.23 V subtraction (for example the largest step at U = 0 given as an overpotential). Keep the authors' number, unit and label; do not correct or endorse it; describe the inconsistency in `eta_note`. If it is unclear that the quantity is a surface-specific CHE result for OER at all, E6 is `UNCLEAR`.
  - **Reported equivalent (v4):** the paper evaluates the largest reaction free-energy step minus 1.23 eV (or the largest step at U = 1.23 V), or states a minimum CHE limiting potential U_L, and the CHE free-energy steps and the surface are identifiable. Check that it is a step between intermediates, not a transition-state barrier. A lone ΔG_RDS at U = 0 that you would have to convert yourself does not count. **[v5 D12]** Neither does a U = 0 step reported only as an "energy barrier", even in volts: units alone do not make it a U_L. Such steps can count only within an author-reported comparison that meets every D4 condition below.
  - **[v5 D13, D4]** A ΔG_max, G_max, ΔG_RDS or ΔG_loss counts as the equivalent only when the paper shows it is the largest single one-electron step between adjacent intermediates. The label "RDS" alone does not show this; the paper's own reported CHE steps can. A free-energy span over several steps does not count. Do not divide a span by e or by a guessed electron count.
  - **[v5 D5]** "At U = 1.23 V" is met when a diagram or its caption is explicitly marked U = 1.23 V and the reported value, in the text or on the diagram, is unambiguously assigned to that diagram, curve and surface; the sentence with the number need not repeat the potential. Several diagrams or curves with an uncertain assignment: `UNCLEAR`. The size of a value never shows its reference potential.
  - **[v5 D5]** Check that the scale is RHE or an explicitly equivalent reference, for example SHE at pH 0. A CHE methods statement that (H⁺ + e⁻) is in equilibrium with ½H2 at U = 0, with no pH shift, defines the RHE scale. The reference may be stated anywhere in the paper or in SI that is in your file. If no reference and no CHE statement is given, the value does not count as the equivalent.
  - **Scaling-derived η** counts when the authors report it for the qualifying surface from their computed inputs.
  - **Graphical, relative or symbolic η** counts when it is explicitly labelled as the authors' CHE result for identifiable surfaces: an η arrow or labelled η on a diagram, volcano or bar plot, a plotted Δη, or "η_A > η_B" as a finding. Never invent a number.
  - **[v5 D4] Relative reported equivalent.** An author-reported comparison or ranking of the potential-determining (largest) CHE reaction steps of two or more surfaces, at one common documented potential (not only 1.23 V, U = 0 included), counts as a relative η when all of these hold:
    - the authors themselves state the comparison or ranking;
    - each compared value is shown in the paper to be that surface's largest step: the authors call it the largest or potential-determining step, or the paper gives the value of every step of that surface's pathway and this one is the largest (a label that the paper's own step values contradict does not count);
    - all steps are conventional one-electron CHE steps;
    - the reference and conditions are the same for all compared surfaces.
    Never build a comparison yourself: a comparison you reconstruct, for example by subtracting or ranking values the authors list but do not compare, does not qualify. Checking which of the paper's listed steps is largest is allowed; computing a new difference is not. A generic "lower barrier" statement, or a lower value of a step not shown to be each surface's largest, does not qualify; surfaces can have different limiting steps. Kinetic barriers, free-energy spans over several steps, different electron counts and constant-potential energetics do not qualify. If these conditions cannot be established, E6 is `UNCLEAR` and the record stays unresolved. Record a qualifying comparison as `eta_form` `relative` + `eta_derivation` `equivalent`.
  - The η must belong to the rutile (110) model. An η given only for another facet, or for a non-rutile component, does not count.
  - None of the following counts as a reported η. If you cannot tell whether a result is one of them, E6 is `UNCLEAR`:
    - ΔG values from which *you* would have to pick a pathway and compute η;
    - a generic "energy barrier" that is neither identified as the CHE maximum step at equilibrium nor part of an author-reported comparison that meets every D4 condition;
    - instructions on how to compute η;
    - an experimental or applied operating overpotential, a current-dependent kinetic overpotential, or an activation barrier;
    - a chosen potential at which the steps happen to be downhill, or a universal volcano-apex optimum;
    - a conceptual sketch, an unlabelled volcano point, or an inequality stated as background;
    - a GC-DFT η alone (grand-canonical DFT is not CHE unless a CHE result is also given).
  - **[v5] Without the SI in your file, E6 is never `NO`**, whatever the other criteria are. If the main text reports no qualifying η, answer `UNCLEAR` and say "in SI" when the main text points to the SI, otherwise "not in main text". With the SI in your file, E6 is `NO` when neither the main text nor the SI reports a qualifying η. **[v5 D10]** A list of SI contents never settles E6, however irrelevant it looks, and a missing *OOH structure does not rule out a scaling-derived η.

## Disposition (exactly one)

- `ELIGIBLE`: all six criteria are `YES`.
- `EXCLUDE`: at least one criterion is clearly `NO`. Give `exclude_criterion` as the **first** failing criterion (E1 to E6), and quote the excerpt that establishes it. E6 can be `NO` only with the SI in your file (see E6).
- `NEEDS_SI`: the SI is not in your file, and either E1–E4 are `YES` and the OER facet is not stated (E5 `UNCLEAR`), or E1–E5 are `YES` and no CHE overpotential is found in the main text. The population counts an overpotential reported in the article *or its SI*, so such a paper is never excluded on E6 from the main text alone.
  - **[v5 D10]** One exception is applied at reconciliation, not in this read: when the publisher's article page shows that no supporting information exists (source and date recorded) and the whole main text, figures and tables included, has been read, E6 is decided from the main text and may be `NO`. SI that exists but cannot be obtained keeps the record unresolved; it is never an E6 exclusion.
- `UNRESOLVED`: anything else that cannot be settled from the text, including a criterion `UNCLEAR` with the SI already in your file, or `UNCLEAR` on E1–E4. Explain in `note`.

A paper whose file is unreadable, truncated or the wrong paper is `UNRESOLVED` with the reason; set `text_ok` to false then, and true otherwise. **Never** exclude for missing access.

## Rules

- Read the paper yourself. Do not write keyword scripts or code that assigns verdicts. Code may be used only to print the text and to check your output file's format.
- Judge each paper only from its own text. Do not use outside lists, other screeners' outputs or memory of the paper.
- **Do not record method details.** Symmetry settings, imaginary-mode or phonon checks, magnetic-state checks and data deposition are out of scope, even if you notice them. The output has no fields for them.
- Keep excerpts verbatim and short.

## Output (JSONL, one line per paper, same order as the batch)

```
{"screen_id": "...", "doi": "...", "text_ok": true,
 "E1": {"v": "YES|NO|UNCLEAR", "where": "...", "excerpt": "..."},
 "E2": {...}, "E3": {...}, "E4": {...}, "E5": {...}, "E6": {...},
 "disposition": "ELIGIBLE|EXCLUDE|NEEDS_SI|UNRESOLVED",
 "exclude_criterion": "E1..E6 or null",
 "form": "journal|preprint|other",
 "eta_form": "numeric|graphical|relative|null",
 "eta_derivation": "direct|equivalent|scaling|null",
 "eta_note": "<= 25 words or null",
 "secondary": "reanalysis|null",
 "provenance": "own|external|mixed|unclear|null",
 "note": "<= 40 words"}
```

- **[v5 D7]** `form` records what kind of text your file is: `journal` for an article in a journal, including its accepted manuscript wherever it is posted (a review or perspective in a journal is `journal`; E1 decides whether it is research); `preprint` for a complete manuscript posted publicly before or without journal acceptance; `other` for a thesis, conference abstract, technical report, book or chapter, or anything else. A report or repository wrapper that contains an identifiable article manuscript takes that manuscript's form.
- **[v5 D14]** `eta_form` records how the qualifying η is presented, and `eta_derivation` how it was obtained; both are null when E6 is not `YES`. `eta_form`: `numeric` when a number for the qualifying surface's η (or its reported equivalent) appears in the text, a table or a figure label; `graphical` when it is shown only as a plotted point, arrow or bar with no number; `relative` when only a difference, comparison or ranking is given (for example "η_A > η_B", a Δη, a D4 comparison). If more than one applies, use the first in that order. `eta_derivation`: `scaling` when the η rests on a stated scaling relation; otherwise `direct` when the authors label it an overpotential (including a D12 inconsistency); otherwise `equivalent` (the largest step minus 1.23 eV, the largest step at U = 1.23 V, a stated U_L, the D5 and D13 cases, a D4 comparison). A scaling-derived η shown only in a figure is `graphical` + `scaling`. These fields describe eligibility evidence only; they are not the reporting-outcome fields.
- `eta_note` records a D12 definition inconsistency; otherwise null.
- `secondary` and `provenance`: see the E3 re-analysis bullet (D11).
- Records read under v4 keep their v4 `eta_form` value on record. Every record with a v4 `eta_form` is read again under v5, which sets both v5 fields from the paper; nothing is copied from an earlier row.

When the disposition is `EXCLUDE`, you may stop evaluating the criteria after the first clear `NO`. Give the remaining criteria as `{"v": "NOT_ASSESSED"}`.
