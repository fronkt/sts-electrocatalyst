## New local policy questions (unadopted)

**SI-Q1 (S16323): scope of a global potential-reference sentence.** Does “All potentials in this work” in a measurement conversion paragraph fix the computational reference when no DFT passage repeats it? Existing Q4 determines which result classes need a reference; this is the separate question of which results the particular statement describes. Recommendation: referential reading of the paragraph, with explicit theoretical reference required when its surrounding sentence defines an SCE-to-RHE measurement conversion. This recommendation is not an adopted exclusion.

**SI-Q2 (S26256): conflicting author ranking and source-data surface assignment.** Does an author-stated largest-step comparison qualify when its source data reverse the surface ordering? D4 forbids reconstructed rankings and rejects a “largest” label contradicted by the paper's own steps, but gives no hierarchy for incompatible surface assignments. Recommendation: E6 UNCLEAR until resolved, without correcting the paper's values or adopting a reader-computed reversal.

**SI-Q3 (S27700): a transformed graphical result.** Does an OER onset line in a pH-dependent CHE Pourbaix plot, captioned as based on theoretical η, itself report graphical η or minimum UL? Recommendation: require the author's explicit η/UL assignment; a relation to η does not make an arbitrary plotted potential an η. This extends the presentation issue beyond Q21's verbal threshold and does not choose an η number.
