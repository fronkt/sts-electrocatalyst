### Q1. For D4's 'common documented potential' and D5's reference scale: what fixes the reference, and must the potential be stated? The cases seen are a named CHE model (for example with G(O2) = 4.92 eV bookkeeping), a ΔG expression with no eU term, and the ½H2 ⇌ H⁺ + e⁻ equilibrium written without 'at U = 0'. In none of them does 'U = 0' or '1.23 V' appear in the text, a caption or a diagram label. The two parts are decided separately.

Q1a. Reference: what fixes the RHE scale under D5?
- (i) D5 as written: the statement that (H⁺ + e⁻) is in equilibrium with ½H2 at U = 0, with no pH shift, or a stated RHE or SHE-at-pH-0 reference.
- (ii) Also the equilibrium written without 'at U = 0'. This relaxes D5.
- (iii) Also a bare CHE name, cited or not. This relaxes D5.
- (iv) A separate case: a stated NHE or SHE reference with no pH given. D5 names 'SHE at pH 0'; accepting an NHE/SHE statement without the pH relaxes D5. This can be answered alongside (i)-(iii).

Q1b. Potential: must the common potential of the compared values be documented?
- (i) Stated: in the text, a caption, or a diagram marked U = 0 or U = 1.23 V. Otherwise E6 is UNCLEAR and the record waits (NEEDS_SI or UNRESOLVED).
- (ii) Inferable: a CHE free-energy scheme with no potential term (a ΔG expression with no eU term, or G(O2) = 4.92 eV bookkeeping) documents U = 0. This is a contestable reading of 'documented'.
- (iii) Not needed. This amends the entrant's D4 condition 'at a common documented potential': 'documented' would apply only to the potential being common, not to its value. If the paper shows that all compared largest steps come from one CHE calculation at one common potential, the D4 condition is met, because a difference between one-electron largest steps is the same at any common potential.

The four options first offered map onto this grid: (a) = Q1a (iii) + Q1b (ii); (b) = Q1a (iii) + Q1b (i); (c) = Q1a (i) + Q1b (i); (d) = Q1b (iii) with the reference left open. No option lets the reader confirm U = 0 from the size of the values (for example steps that sum to 4.92 eV): D5 says 'The size of a value never shows its reference potential', and D4 does not allow computing a new quantity.

Recommendation (for the entrant to decide): Q1a (i) and Q1b (i). D5 names the statement that 'defines the RHE scale'; (ii) and (iii) drop parts of it, so accepting a named CHE framework relaxes D5 rather than reading it. D4 as adopted requires the potential to be 'documented', and the entrant's D4 condition says unclear conditions leave the record unresolved. D5 also says 'the size of a value never shows its reference potential', which rules out reading U = 0 off the numbers. Decide Q1a together with Q4b: an answer that accepts a bare CHE name here fits Q4b yes. Q4b yes accepts a weaker signal than Q1a (ii), so it implies at least Q1a (ii); Q1a (i) with Q4b yes is not a coherent combination. On (iv), the recommendation is no: D5 names SHE at pH 0, and an NHE statement with no pH does not fix the scale.

Closest v5 text (E6 [v5 D4] relative reported equivalent (lead sentence); E6 [v5 D5] reference bullet): "An author-reported comparison or ranking of the potential-determining (largest) CHE reaction steps of two or more surfaces, at one common documented potential (not only 1.23 V, U = 0 included), counts as a relative η … A CHE methods statement that (H⁺ + e⁻) is in equilibrium with ½H2 at U = 0, with no pH shift, defines the RHE scale."

### Q2. Suppose a paper identifies the computed model as rutile explicitly (by the D1 or D6 route) but elsewhere uses one phase label with a non-rutile identifier. The label may name another polymorph, or put a non-rutile space group inside a label that itself says rutile, and it is not shown to refer to the computed model. Is that 'contradictory phase information' that leaves E4 UNCLEAR?

Options: (a) No. Explicit identification of the model as rutile prevails over an isolated conflicting label. The label is recorded in the note as a probable slip, and E4 is YES.; (b) Yes. Any conflicting label that could refer to the computed model, its parent or its characterized sample is a contradiction. E4 is UNCLEAR (UNRESOLVED) until the paper resolves it.; (c) Decide by referent. The label contradicts only when it is attached to the computed model, or to the material whose rutile evidence is used for the model. A label on a separately prepared reference sample does not. A label whose referent is unclear is settled by reading the item it cites (public SI is allowed), and the record is held UNRESOLVED until then.

Recommendation (for the entrant to decide): (c). D6 says 'Judge what a sentence refers to, not where it sits', and D1 forbids carrying phase evidence across components. By the same logic, a conflicting label should count only against what it refers to.

Closest v5 text (E4 [v5 D6] bullet; E4 [v5 D1] bullet): "Judge what a sentence refers to, not where it sits. An introduction or methods sentence that identifies the material actually modelled as rutile counts when it is clearly connected to the model and nothing contradicts it … and no contradictory phase information appears."
