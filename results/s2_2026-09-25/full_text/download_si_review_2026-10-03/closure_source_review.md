## Source-specific adjudication notes

**S28435 D12 question — frozen third-read output unchanged.** Main p. 6 calls the `η = 0.50 eV` quantity a reduction in an “activation barrier”; p. 7 calls the paired 0.47/0.50 eV results “reaction energy barriers.” SI p. 5 says four OER steps are estimated and gives `ΔG=ΔE+ΔEZPE−TΔS−eU`, but does not identify the quoted η as a reported CHE overpotential or specify a facet. v5 D12 is narrower: it accepts a *surface-specific CHE quantity the authors themselves label overpotential*, while the E6 exclusions expressly exclude activation barriers unless they meet D4. The η symbol alone does not supply the required label/result identity. Further, v5 says the η must belong to the qualifying rutile(110) model; this paper's DFT descriptions name Co3O4-RuO2 but no computed facet, so the quoted number cannot be assigned to rutile(110) on this source. **Recommendation to root:** the current E6 YES is not source-secure; UNCLEAR is the conservative v5 reading, and the entrant question is likely answered by the existing E6/D12 text rather than a policy gap. Do not change the frozen row here.

**S29447 D4 source check.** Main p. 7 identifies 20 `Ru1−xIrxO2(110)` slabs and 62 cus sites, states that CHE cannot calculate activation energies, and says the maximum of ΔG1–ΔG4 at 0 V vs RHE is each site's limiting potential. SI Table S2 gives the four successive one-electron OER reaction steps with `−eU`; its method paragraph defines CHE against H2 at U vs RHE. SI Table S3 reports each site's `max ΔGi` at 0 V vs RHE and says the most active Ir and Ru cus sites are highlighted. Original DOCX table shading confirms two highlighted rows: x=0.29, Ir cus, max ΔGi=1.46 eV (surface 0); x=0.58, Ru cus, max ΔGi=1.70 eV (surface 4). Main pp. 9–10 compare Ru–Ir–O activity against pure IrO2(110)/RuO2(110), and p. 10 says highest activity at a “low theoretical overpotential of 1.5 V” requires both elements.

**Recommendation to root:** the strongest conservative E6 route is D4 (author-highlighted potential-determining step comparison), not kinetic-barrier language: the steps are one-electron, their maxima and shared U=0/RHE basis are expressly tabulated, and the paper identifies the highlighted sites as most active. Keep the comparison attached to the modeled rutile(110) alloy-site family; do not infer that an experimental activity curve or a generic barrier alone is η. Whether the highlighted Ir/Ru-site selections plus the main-text ranking meet D4's “author-reported comparison or ranking” wording is the remaining human judgment; no new policy is proposed here.
