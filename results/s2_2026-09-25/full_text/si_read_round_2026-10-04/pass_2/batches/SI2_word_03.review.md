# Word pass 2 review, batch word_03

Coverage: for each of the five papers I read the main text in full, in consecutive chunks of at most 12,000 characters (up to the "reference list removed" marker, or for S29795 through the reference list and the closing declaration page), and the primary rendering of the SI text in full in the same way. Each SI text file also holds a second, Pandoc rendering of the same Word file. I compared its words and numbers against the primary rendering by script and found no text that is absent from the primary rendering (only layout widths and table borders). Main-text figures were available only as captions and printed labels: the `visual_root` folders hold SI media only, so no main-text figure image exists to open. I inspected every contact sheet of each assigned `visual_root/contacts` folder and opened original media for the deciding items listed per paper. Movies were inventoried only (S30575 Movies S1-S3, not viewed). The text was never truncated and no SI was incomplete (`si_complete` true for all five). A scratch script checked that every excerpt is a verbatim substring of the main or SI text (whitespace, soft-hyphen and line-end hyphen normalised); it assigned no verdicts.

S23308 (Catalysis Today, Man and Tranca) — EXCLUDE on E6. Read: main text chunks 0, 12000, 24000, 36000, 48000 (to the reference marker at 54,154); SI text (312 characters, paragraphs 1-3). Contact sheet: `contacts/word_01.png`. Original media: `docx_media/image1.emf.png` (the rendered preview of the SI's only table, "ZPE and TS corrections"; the EMF itself cannot be opened). The SI table lists zero-point and entropy corrections per source article and no free energies or overpotentials. The paper mostly re-analyses adsorption energies collected from the literature (the Rossmeisl-group dataset), which is reused data and not a new calculation. It also runs new RPBE and RPBE+U calculations of HO*/O*/HOO* on doped and charged rutile TiO2(110) (2x4 cell), so E3, E4 and E5 are YES for that model. For those new calculations the authors state "Since the final value of the overpotential is not the purpose of this case study, we do not extend further the discussion concerning the theoretical overpotentials", and Figs. 3-4 and their captions give only scaling lines. The literature-derived η_TD values in Fig. 2 are binned across many materials and are not tied to a rutile (110) model. With the complete SI in the file and the whole text read, E6 is NO. I did not set `secondary`, because the paper does contain new qualifying calculations; the note records that most of the paper is reuse.
