## S00882 (10.1016/j.electacta.2013.11.027) - EXCLUDE (E6)

Read: main text chunks 0, 12000, 24000, 36000 (reference list removed at char 42062); SI reading text chunks 0 and 12000 (16,443 chars, complete). No separate original image was opened: the sheet shows the whole figure at readable size, with CER/OER boundary lines and surface labels but no free-energy steps or overpotentials.

Disposition: the paper is a constrained ab initio thermodynamics (surface Pourbaix) study of the chlorine evolution reaction on RuO2(110). I counted this as E3 YES (OER analysed as a competing reaction, v5 D3) and E5 YES (2x1 RuO2(110) slab). E4 is UNCLEAR: rutile is never named in the main text or SI, only the RuO2(110) label. E6 is NO: the main text and the complete SI (blocks 4-26: dG0 tables for 116 Pourbaix structures, free-enthalpy equations, Fig. SI) contain no OER free-energy steps, no limiting potential and no overpotential. First clearly failing criterion: E6.
