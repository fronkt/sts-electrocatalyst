Disposition paragraph: E1-E5 are YES. The paper (available online 6 July 2024) computes OER on "RuO2(101)/TiO2(101) and RuO2(110)/TiO2(101) slab models", the latter representing NS-RuO2-250 whose exposed RuO2 (110) facets are the subject of the (110) analysis, and states "lattice parameters of rutile RuO2 and TiO2" for the models. E6 is unresolved. Fig. 4d,e (captioned "at 0 V vs. RHE") is described in the text only as AEM RDS "*OOH formation ... energy barrier (1.59 eV)" for the (101) model and, for the (110) model, a LOM pathway that is "thermodynamically favored but still exhibited a high energy barrier (1.91 eV), 0.32 eV larger". The SI holds no step values. The figure images were not available, so labelled values on them could not be checked.
