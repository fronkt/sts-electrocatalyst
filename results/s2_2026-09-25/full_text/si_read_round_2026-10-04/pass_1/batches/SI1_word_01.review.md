S00882 — EXCLUDE (E6). A constrained-thermodynamics (surface Pourbaix) study of the chlorine evolution reaction on RuO2(110). OER appears only as a competing reaction: the OER line is marked on the diagrams, (O2)ot is called an OER precursor but is found unstable to desorption, and 2Obr+2Oot is named the active phase under OER conditions. I took that as enough for E3 (partial cycle, explicit OER link). Rutile is never named (formula and CUS labels only), so E4 is unclear. The whole main text, the SI tables (only per-structure ΔG0 and stoichiometric coefficients) and the one SI image (a Pourbaix diagram with CER/OER boundaries and no overpotential) contain no OER free-energy steps or overpotential, and the SI is complete, so E6 is NO. Publication date: "Available online 18 November 2013".

S00887 — EXCLUDE (E6). The DFT reports water adsorption energies, O-H bond-breaking reaction energies and CINEB barriers only (no CHE free-energy steps, no potential dependence, no computed overpotential); "overpotential" occurs only for experiments. The SI (tables S1 to S4 and Fig. S1 kinetic-parameter schematic, image inspected) holds no further OER energetics. Form recorded as journal (author-manuscript wrapper); this is uncertain.
