S00358 (Karlsson, Cornell, Pettersson, Electrochim. Acta 2015) — EXCLUDE on E6. E1-E5 are YES: a primary DFT screening of 38 dopants in rutile TiO2, available online 21 August 2015; OER intermediates are treated through the O adsorption energy E(Oc) on the (110) surface, explicitly as the OER/ClER descriptor, with the O* intermediate studied during OER (Sec. 3.4). The 1x1 cell is described as one bridge and one cus site and the descriptor is defined "on the (110) surface of a rutile oxide". The whole main text and the whole SI were read. The paper never computes CHE free-energy steps or reports any overpotential. It places the computed E(Oc) values against the Hansen volcano and its apex ("descriptor value for maximum OER activity"), and SI Figs. 7 and 8 (original p. 10 opened, p. 11 seen on the contact sheet) are bar plots of E(Oc) with those horizontal reference lines and no eta label. A volcano-apex optimum is not a reported eta under the instructions, and the SI is complete, so E6 is NO.
