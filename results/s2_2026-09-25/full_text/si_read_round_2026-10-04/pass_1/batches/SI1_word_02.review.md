Disposition: a Research Square preprint (version 1, posted 6 Dec 2023) with its own DFT work. The SI gives the four-step *OH/*O/*OOH CHE pathway on a RuO2 slab with the (110) surface, with Zn@RuO2 and CoZn@RuO2 built from that slab (Figs. S10, S25-S28 confirm slab and adsorbates). I took P4_2/mnm for RuO2 as the rutile-type structure (D9) and that sentence as the D1 connection. The XRD also shows hexagonal Ru metal, a separate component that does not contradict the RuO2 phase. E6: the main text states a limiting potential ("limited potential") of 1.75 eV for Zn@RuO2 and 1.66 eV for CoZn@RuO2, and the SI defines U_L = ΔG0/e on the RHE scale with the four CHE steps, so I counted a reported equivalent. The main text also mentions theoretical-overpotential plots (Figs. 5e, 7b), which I could not see. The reported model is CoZn@RuO2 (110).

Disposition: Applied Catalysis B article, available online 6 July 2024, with its own Quantum ESPRESSO DFT on two slab models: RuO2(101)/TiO2(101), representing NL-RuO2-250, and RuO2(110)/TiO2(101), representing NS-RuO2-250. Sec. 2.7 states that rutile RuO2 and TiO2 lattice parameters were used, and the Sec. 3.1 text ties NS-RuO2-250 to dominantly exposed (110) facets. The NL model is a (101) surface, so it fails E5. The NS model is the reported model, with E3 to E5 YES. For E6 the only number tied to NS is a "high energy barrier (1.91 eV)" for the LOM pathway, 0.32 eV above NL's AEM RDS (1.59 eV), in diagrams drawn at 0 V vs. RHE. The text does not call the NS value the largest step, gives no AEM value for NS, and compares an LOM path with an AEM path on a different facet, so the D4 conditions are not established and a U = 0 "energy barrier" does not count by itself (D12). I left E6 UNCLEAR rather than NO; the SI has no further DFT values. The disposition follows as UNRESOLVED.
