## S20599 — ELIGIBLE — entrant question

**Inspected:** main contact sheets 01–02 (pp. 1–7). Full-resolution page: p. 5 (Fig. 5a–e). Figs. 1–4 (synthesis, HRTEM/HAADF with RuO2(110)/(101) fringes, XRD/XPS/EPR, electrochemistry) were read on the contact sheet. SI: contacts `word_01.png`, `word_02.png` and `word_wide_01.png`, covering Figs. S1–S7 and Fig. S8 (model structures of the RuO2 and Ce-RuO2 slab, *OH, *O and *OOH).

**Disposition:** The reported model is Ce-doped RuO2(110). Its parent phase is identified as rutile in the paper's own words, "rutile RuO2 (No. 40–1290)", with no CeO2 reflections. The DFT is introduced as examining "the impact of the introduced Ce dopant" in the synthesized catalyst, which documents the doped model's link to the rutile parent (E4 YES). Fig. 5b, seen for the first time here, is a free-energy diagram marked "U=1.23 V". The text places it "at the equilibrium potential (1.23 VDFT-RHE)", which gives the RHE scale.
- **RuO2 (navy):** levels 0 → ≈ −0.92 → −1.05 → −0.28 → 0. The labelled 0.77 is the *O→*OOH step and plainly the largest.
- **RuCeO2 (orange):** levels 0 → ≈ +0.23 → −0.53 → −0.22 → 0. The labelled 0.31 is the *O→*OOH step, larger than the other uphill steps of about 0.23 and 0.22.

So the 0.31 eV value is the Ce-RuO2 largest step at U = 1.23 V, a numeric equivalent η, and E6 is YES (numeric/equivalent). This matches the abstract and conclusions ("0.31 vs. 0.77 eV"). The text's "reduce … by 0.31" is a wording slip that the diagram resolves. SI Fig. S8 shows the authors built the pristine RuO2 models too, so the [49,50] citation beside 0.77 does not make that curve external. The pristine model would also qualify.

**Versus the passes:** Both passes had E6 UNCLEAR because the Fig. 5b image was unavailable. With the page image, E6 is YES and the disposition changes from UNRESOLVED to ELIGIBLE. **Entrant question:** does reading the unlabelled step heights off Fig. 5b satisfy D13's "paper shows it is the largest step" when only the RDS values are printed?

**Disposition:** There are two computed models. The Ru-RuO2 heterostructure is "built using RuO2 (101) and Ru (100)", so it is not (110) and E5 is NO for it. The reported model is therefore the pristine RuO2 benchmark.
- **E3 YES.**
- **E4 UNCLEAR:** card 88-0286 is rutile-type per the reconciliation fact. However, the pure RuO2 model is presented only as a comparison ("compared with RuO2"), and the paper never documents that it represents the characterized RuO2 phase.
- **E5 UNCLEAR:** the main text says "optimized RuO2 (110)" (also in the k-point setup), while the SI Fig. S32 caption says "pure RuO2 (111)".
- **E6 UNCLEAR:** SI Fig. S31b, seen at full size, is marked "U=0 V". Under D5, a value with no reference and no CHE statement does not count as the equivalent.

E2 follows pass 1 (UNCLEAR) against pass 2 (YES), since the 2024 date is the journal version's. The SI images were what decided E6; the main-text figures changed nothing. **Entrant question:** does D5's missing-reference rule block a D4 comparison at "U=0 V" when the paper gives no CHE or RHE reference for its DFT but writes the four AEM steps as (H+ + e−) transfers?
