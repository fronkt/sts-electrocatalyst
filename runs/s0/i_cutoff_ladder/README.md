## Scoring recipe (exact)

1. QC per job — success is a final total energy AND no convergence failure
   (`JOB DONE` alone is NEVER success):
   ```bash
   cd /workspace/sts/runs/s0/i_cutoff_ladder
   for j in tio2 sno2; do for e in 60 80 100 120; do f=${j}__ecut${e}.out; \
     printf '%s  scf_fail=%s  E=%s\n' "$f" \
       "$(grep -ac 'convergence NOT achieved' $f)" \
       "$(grep -a '^!' $f | tail -1)"; done; done
   ```
   Every job must show scf_fail=0 and a `!    total energy` line. A missing/failed
   rung is rerun before scoring; a rung that cannot converge is itself a recorded
   capability result for that oxide.
2. Admission delta per oxide (REGISTERED test): |E(80) - E(100)| < 0.002205 Ry
   (= 30 meV/cell = 5 meV/atom, per-atom normalisation registered — divide by 6):
   ```bash
   python3 - <<'EOF'
   import re
   RY2MEV = 13605.693123
   for ox in ("tio2", "sno2"):
       E = {}
       for e in (60, 80, 100, 120):
           txt = open(f"{ox}__ecut{e}.out", errors="ignore").read()
           E[e] = float(re.findall(r"^!\s+total energy\s+=\s+(-?\d+\.\d+)", txt, re.M)[-1])
       d = abs(E[80] - E[100]) * RY2MEV
       print(f"{ox}: |E80-E100| = {d:.2f} meV/cell = {d/6:.3f} meV/atom ->",
             "PASS" if d < 30.0 else "FAIL", "(threshold 30 meV/cell = 0.002205 Ry)")
       for e in (60, 80, 100, 120):   # context table vs the 120 Ry point
           print(f"  {e:>3} Ry: E = {E[e]:.8f} Ry   dE/atom vs 120 Ry = "
                 f"{(E[e]-E[120])*RY2MEV/6:+.3f} meV")
   EOF
   ```
   The 60/120 rows are context (monotonic approach to the asymptote expected);
   they carry no threshold.

## Recorded on pass / on fail (per requirements.md, restated)

- PASS (per oxide): the full 4-point E(ecut) ladder is recorded; the endmember's
  pseudo is qualified inside the frozen 80/640 protocol. TiO2: admitted to the tier
  (last admission requirement for n=8). SnO2: capability recorded, admission stays
  PENDING the A7.5 condition.
- FAIL (per oxide): the ladder and the failure margin are recorded; the
  pseudopotential is NOT qualified at 80/640 and the endmember does not enter the
  tier under the frozen protocol. Any rescue (different pseudo, raised per-metal
  cutoff exception) is a recorded upstream DEVIATION decision, never a silent change.

Downstream: S3's TiO2 addition (n=8); the conditional SnO2 9th member; gate (g)
does not formally wait on (i), but its tier relevance does.
