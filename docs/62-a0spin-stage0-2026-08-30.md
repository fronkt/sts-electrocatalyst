**One-paragraph summary.** All ten Stage-0 jobs converged. The eight P11 reproduction
decks reproduce their banked energies to **≤ 3.21e-6 Ry (0.044 meV)** — and the derived
quantity that actually decides the arm, Δc_M(0), to **≤ 0.052 meV against a 15.5 meV

## 1. What Stage 0 was for

banked P11 nspin = 2 energies (doubling as the campaign's only determinism control on a
spin-polarised code path), and two null-seed decks — deliberately **one of each `ntyp`
class**, so the state-dependent species-index rule is exercised on both branches — must
reproduce the banked nspin = 1 Ti energies at totmag ≈ 0.

**The index rule held.** Read from the decks as run: `slab` / `s0_O` (`ntyp = 2`, `[M, O]`)
carry `starting_magnetization(1) = 0.50` and `(2) = 0.0`; `s0_OH` / `s0_OOH`

seeded and only the metal, on all eight decks, across both classes. The blocker
`build_a0spin.py` exists to prevent did not occur.

## 5. Control set 2 — the null-seed decks, and the criterion that cannot be met

Both Ti decks ran with **every** `starting_magnetization` set to `0.0`, `nspin = 2`,
`HUBBARD (atomic) U Ti-3d 9.0000`, `conv_thr = 1.0d-6`.

| deck | electrons | totmag | absmag | ΔE vs banked nspin = 1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ti `slab` | 144 (**even**) | −0.00 | 0.00 | **+0.339 meV** |
| Ti `s0_OOH` | 157 (**ODD**) | **1.04** | 1.25 | **−153.072 meV** |

**The even-electron deck behaves exactly as the repo claims.** `qe_slab.py` states that
`nspin = 2` with every seed at zero is a fixed point of the SCF that reproduces the
nspin = 1 answer at twice the cost. On the Ti slab, totmag reads −0.00 at every one of the
25 iterations and the energy lands within 0.339 meV (25× `conv_thr`, and the residual is
the doubled-spin occupation machinery, not a moment). **The fixed-point claim is confirmed
— for closed-shell decks.**

**The odd-electron deck broke symmetry on its own.** The totmag trajectory of
`Ti/s0_OOH__u900__sp2null.out` runs 0.01, 0.00, 0.10, 0.20, −0.03, 0.00, 0.00, **0.43**,
**1.00**, 1.00, … and locks at 1.04 over 47 iterations. Nothing seeded it. The only
symmetry-breaking source in the deck is QE's own `Starting wfcs are 117 randomized atomic
wfcs`. From that noise alone the SCF fell **153.07 meV** below the banked nspin = 1 row.

Three things follow, in increasing order of importance.

1. **The registered criterion is unsatisfiable as written, and this is a correction of
   record, not a build failure.** docs/61 §A11.7 requires *both* null-seed decks to
   "reproduce the banked nspin = 1 Ti energies with totmag ≈ 0". On an odd-electron state
   whose unpolarised solution is *unstable*, no converged `nspin = 2` run can satisfy that.
   The `ntyp = 3` half of control set 2 must be re-registered as what it actually is (§5.2)
   before Stage 1 is scored against it.
2. **What it measured instead is a spin-stability test, and the nspin = 1 Ti \*OOH solution
   at U = 9 FAILS it.** This is the sharper form of docs/60 §4's closing sentence — "the
   residual open question is the spin convention, not the geometry" — with a number on it.
3. **153.07 meV is a lower bound, not an estimate.** No moment was ever requested; the run
   found whichever branch numerical noise pointed at, under none of the registered seed
   search. A seeded search can only go lower.

### 5.1 The parity is structural, and it lands on exactly the two states c_M is built from

Read from the as-built decks, all three nspin = 1 metals:

| state | Ru | Ir | Ti | parity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `slab` | 168 | 162 | 144 | even |
| `s0_O` | 174 | 168 | 150 | even |
| `s0_OH` | 175 | 169 | 151 | **odd** |
| `s0_OOH` | 181 | 175 | 157 | **odd** |

c_M = ΔG_OOH − ΔG_OH is built from `s0_OH` and `s0_OOH` and **nothing else survives the
referencing**. The two even-electron states are exactly the two that cancel. So on all
three nspin = 1 metals, **every** term entering A7.3's quantity is an odd-electron state
described as a closed shell. docs/61 §A11.8 item 1 corrected the caveat from "a Ti problem"
to "a three-metal problem"; §5.1 sharpens it again — it is a problem confined to, and
unavoidable in, the numerator.

### 5.2 Proposed re-registration of the `ntyp = 3` null-seed control (entrant's to authorise)

The control's *purpose* — exercise the metal-at-index-2 path on a deck where a per-metal
constant would seed oxygen — was served: the deck ran, seeded index 2 with 0.0, and
converged. Its *acceptance criterion* was not. **PROPOSED** replacement, in two parts:

- **(a) index-rule leg:** the deck's `starting_magnetization(2)` must sit on the metal
  species read from its own `ATOMIC_SPECIES` block. **PASSES** as run.
- **(b) stability leg, reported not scored:** the run is a null-seed spin-stability probe.
  If totmag stays ≈ 0 the unpolarised solution is stable and the banked nspin = 1 row is its
  own ground state; if it breaks, the magnitude is recorded as a **lower bound** on the
  polarised gain and the affected nspin = 1 row is labelled SPIN-UNSTABLE. **Ti `s0_OOH`
  at U = 9.0: BREAKS, ≥ 153.07 meV, SPIN-UNSTABLE.**

## 6. Why this reorders the arm: the live metal is Ti

docs/61 was written around Ru. §A11.0 opens on Ru's 15.5 meV, §A11.1's whole reframe is
built to stop a large spin effect being read as a large score change, and §A11.10 sequences
**Ru first, Ir second, Ti last**. Stage 0 says the information is at the other end.

- Ti needs **D_Ti ≤ −112.5 meV** to cross the 0.10 V floor (docs/60 §6, distance column;
  Δ[span/2] = −D_M/2 and Ti sits 0.0438 V against 0.10 V).
- One of the four state-endpoint legs of D_Ti is now measured at **−153.07 meV**, i.e.
  **larger than the whole distance Ti has to travel**, and it is a lower bound.
- Ru's entire measured spin effect on c_M at U = 0 is **+7.094 meV**. Ti's single-state
  effect at U = 9 is **~22× larger**.

**What this does NOT license, and the reframe is why.** A11.1 is arithmetic:
D_M = Δc_M(U_max) − Δc_M(0), and **a U-independent offset cancels exactly**. Three of
D_Ti's four terms are unrun — Ti \*OH at U = 9, and both states at U = 0. If Ti \*OH at U = 9
shifts comparably, Δc_M(9) is small; if Ti \*OOH at U = 0 shifts comparably, D_Ti is small.
**Stage 0 licenses no statement about D_Ti's sign or magnitude.** What it establishes is
that the *scale* of the spin effect on Ti is ~150 meV rather than the ~10 meV seen on
Ru/Ir at U = 0, so Ti's leg of the arm is a live measurement and not a formality, and the
cancellation — not the size — is the entire question. Exactly as registered, now on a
metal nobody was watching.

**The governance consequence is the awkward one.** Ti is the half of the arm gated on
docs/59 §3c, which is **not countersigned**. docs/61 §A11.10 sequenced Ti last on a
governance argument, and that argument is unchanged and still correct — physics interest
does not license spending SCFs on rows that may be WITHDRAWN-UNSCORED. But decision item 4
has moved from bookkeeping onto the critical path: it now gates the most informative
measurement in the arm, against an Oct 15 hard freeze.
