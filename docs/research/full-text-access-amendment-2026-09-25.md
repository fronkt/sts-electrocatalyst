# Full-text stage: access and procedure decisions — 2026-09-25

These are the entrant's decisions of 2026-09-25, made in session and recorded before the routes named here were used for the full-text stage. They amend the access description in `literature-access-review-2026-09-20.md`. That record says only ordinary, author/institution and institutional-subscription routes were used, and that no bypass was attempted. Earlier batches remain as recorded.

1. **Access routes.** Tried in order, and every attempt is logged per record:
   1. OpenAlex open-access locations.
   2. Unpaywall. The contact address is sent with the request, as the API requires. It is redacted from committed logs.
   3. Europe PMC full text.
   4. arXiv and ChemRxiv.
   5. Purdue University Libraries institutional access. It runs in a dedicated browser window after the entrant logs in once.
   6. Sci-Hub, as the last fallback. The entrant authorised it ("Go ahead and access Sci-hub").

   Each retrieved file records the route that supplied it. Files stay local and are never committed or redistributed. Only the log and hashes are public. A record that no route reaches stays UNRESOLVED_ACCESS, as before. The route that supplied a paper does not affect its eligibility or coding.
2. **Eligibility reading (D2).** Both full-text eligibility passes are made by independent in-session screeners reading the full text. Neither pass sees the other, and the batching differs between them. The entrant decides the interpretive cases at reconciliation. Coding of the four method fields stays the entrant's alone (43:1906).
3. **Non-article forms (D3).** OPS:68 limits the population to primary research *articles*. The rule:
   - A conference abstract, conference paper, dissertation, report or dataset is carried only when version linking ties it to a journal article. The group is then read once, through the article.
   - A form with no linked article is excluded under E1 (not a research article).
   - That exclusion gets the same independent second review as every other exclusion.
   - The excluded forms are listed with their DOIs.
