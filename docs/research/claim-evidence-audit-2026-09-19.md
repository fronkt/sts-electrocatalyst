## Keep measurement, freedom and consequence separate

The detector's external observation is a header-supported persistent exact-zero lateral-force signature under its stated atom-selection and printed-precision rules. “LOCKED” is that operational verdict. A not-LOCKED verdict does not establish exploration of every physically relevant direction, and a LOCKED verdict alone does not establish a saddle, a wrong adsorption energy or an erroneous paper conclusion. The in-house controls validate instrument behavior in their own tested conditions; they cannot supply an unmeasured energetic penalty to every Xu flag.

The four-layer class is therefore a useful location for the observed pattern, not its isolated causal variable. The absence of detections in the two-layer class does not show that two-layer models are safer or more accurate. Both classes retain symmetry-enabled settings; their different recognized operations and atomic/model arrangements require a controlled mechanism test if a causal layer-count statement is desired.

S6 gives separate in-house parameter sensitivity. The scaling identity and a measured limiting-step change should not be joined to the external detection count as though every flagged Xu output has the same consequence. No general coverage-consequence finding is available from the present P-SYMCOV readout, so the worksheet's C7 note about coverage conditionality belonging in following sentences must not become an affirmative coverage result.

## Two different mathematical limits in the S6 wording

The registered P-FLOOR-U metric is the fixed-endpoint quantity `|c(Umax)-c(0)|/2`, not the largest range discovered anywhere in the grid. The general CHE floor is `F(c)=max(c,4.92-c)/2-1.23 V`. Equating the endpoint metric to `|F(c(Umax))-F(c(0))|` requires attention to the branch change at c=2.46 eV. The banked Mn endpoint crosses from c=3.6172 to 2.3558 eV, and Fe from 3.5181 to 2.2977 eV, so their endpoint metric is not simply the change of this general floor.

A separate condition governs `eta=(c/2-1.23)+|DeltaG2-DeltaG3|/2`: it represents the full overpotential only when the limiting step is 2 or 3. Mn and Fe high-U endpoints have limiting step 1. The branch condition for the general floor and the limiting-step condition for the overpotential decomposition should be explained separately; neither changes the registered 3/6 score. The existing banked fields already expose these endpoint values and `closed_form_runs_at_endpoints=false` for Mn/Fe.

## Recommendations for the worksheet's dated addendum

1. Correct its C10 count from 70 LOCKED to 50 LOCKED, while preserving 70/810 as the distinct header count. Put the original/retained adsorbate denominators and unknown accounting beside the central claim's evidence.
2. Treat “identifies” as an operational detection statement, with the exact-zero/header rule explicit. Do not describe a universal physical constraint prevalence or a universal sensitivity/specificity bound.
3. Add the observed-class/causal-thickness distinction and the four unconverged named-pair members. Retain the failed combined P-XU prediction beside the held 10/10 subclause.
4. Retain P-PLS 5/6 and P-FLOOR-U 3/6 as separate outcomes with their robustness and fixed-geometry limits. The “floor result” is not an unqualified positive consequence claim; distinguish the two mathematical conditions above.
5. Preserve the unresolved branches. P-XU-SPAN has one complete above-threshold ladder and four incomplete ladders, but Cr, Mn and Re already have above-threshold observed lower bounds; it is not evidence that only one metal is U-sensitive. P-LIT remains uncoded, and unavailable matched gas references do not supply a completed Xu floor-margin result.

With these corrections, the completed evidence supports a bounded detector/corpus measurement with an explicitly failed high-incidence prediction and separate in-house parameter-sensitivity findings. That is a scope recommendation for the scheduled re-test, not its final disposition.
