## Work that can proceed before enumeration finishes

The operating rule requires discovery and a frozen inclusion/exclusion list **before method coding**. It does not prohibit prospective eligibility triage of already discovered candidates. Keep this triage additive and provisional until the final discovery union closes. For each candidate retain discovery occurrences, reconciled identity, title/abstract triage, the primary-source evidence for surface/OER/CHE/date/type criteria, inspected file hashes and locators, and an explicit inclusion, exclusion or unresolved disposition. Do not populate symmetry, imaginary-mode, magnetism or raw-output-deposition fields, or tally the hypothesis while screening. Reading enough methods/SI to establish eligibility does not authorize recording those outcome fields early.

A title/abstract that omits `(110)` or CHE is insufficient to exclude a potentially relevant calculation; inspect the primary article/SI. Clearly irrelevant metadata can support a logged pre-screening disposition, but every final exclusion requires the registered second independent review. Unavailable evidence remains unresolved rather than a negative result. Absence from the local access inventory is not proof of inaccessible full text.

Reconcile the fixed Xu/Man/Divanis/synthesis leads with the growing OpenAlex union. Inspect references once from each eligible **base-discovery** paper, retaining parent identity, reference number and discovery generation. References discovered only through that pass do not seed further expansion; that would be recursive. A paper later also found independently in a registered base stream is a base paper and receives its own once-only check. Preserve that distinction so the order of screening does not change which references enter. No forward-citation arm is registered. DOI validation may run as included candidates are identified, with a final sweep after reconciliation.

## Stable enumeration: what must change and what cannot be claimed

Continuing q3/q4 after the permitted boundary is necessary. It cannot repair q1/q2's terminal count drift: the existing engine revalidates their cached pages and retains the same anomaly. Repeatedly invoking the current driver will not turn those fixed receipts into COMPLETE_API_ENUMERATION. The supervisor also cannot infer scientific completion from an exit code or an API-exhausted marker.

Under the current engine, a clean stream requires exhausted pagination, one unchanged reported total matching all returned records, verified provider IDs without duplicates, consistent cache/continuation evidence and unchanged pinned source. All four streams must meet this for `database_search_complete=true`. The amendment additionally requires the historical anomalies to remain disclosed; deduplication repairs the candidate union but does not establish enumeration completeness.

A feasible remediation is an **additive, prospectively specified reconciliation run** after preserving the present crawl. The smallest unchanged-interface attempt is a fresh complete crawl of each anomalous query, followed by provider-ID set comparison and retention of the union. It must start at its own first cursor rather than inherit the anomalous pages as proof of completion. A clean later pass supplies evidence about its own retrieval interval; it does not retroactively validate the first crawl.

If live relevance paging continues to drift across daily budgets, prepare and bank an explicit operational amendment before changing retrieval order or partitioning: keep all four literal queries and the whole registered window, cover the window with a complete disjoint calendar-date partition, specify in advance how unstable partitions subdivide, and use documented supported sorting/cursor behavior. Preserve per-partition receipts, totals and full identity sets, reconcile every old-only/new-only ID, and explicitly specify the evidence required to close an anomaly. Do not quietly accept a stable-looking subset, delete old-only candidates, or invent a new numerical stopping tolerance after observing results. This audit does not adopt a particular number of repeat passes or a relaxed drift threshold.

OpenAlex documents cursor continuation with a supported maximum page size of 100; basic paging stops at 10,000 records. It documents publication-date sorting and multiple sort keys, and from/to publication-date filters. These support a bounded prospective enumeration design, but the documentation does **not** establish an immutable snapshot or guarantee that date sorting alone removes ties, additions or metadata changes. A stable provider-ID set across independent passes is evidence of stability, not proof of universal literature coverage. [Paging](https://help.openalex.org/api/paging/), [sorting](https://help.openalex.org/api/sorting/), [date filters](https://help.openalex.org/api/filtering/).

A bulk snapshot would be a different retrieval implementation. Reproducing the API's full-text search semantics against it is an additional validation problem, so downloading a snapshot is not automatically an equivalent shortcut. No paid access or changed provider identity is needed to carry out the current registered continuation; neither is a remedy for the scientific count-drift evidence.

## Exact completion boundaries

1. **Database enumeration closed:** all four literal-query streams have complete, auditable pagination under the registered procedure, or a prospective reconciliation amendment has explicitly resolved their anomalies. Preserve each original drift/failure record, all candidate identities and the full retrieval interval. Do not change the current readout's false completeness flag by hand.
2. **Registered discovery closed:** add and reconcile every named complementary source; finish the once-only reference pass on each included base paper; account for every resulting candidate and discovery edge. OpenAlex alone cannot close this boundary.
3. **Eligibility list frozen:** deduplicate DOI and justified title/year/first-author identities, resolve version/date conflicts, retain every inclusion/exclusion reason and access limitation, and finish independent review of each included row and exclusion. DOI-specific Crossref validation and unresolved metadata must be explicit. An unresolved eligibility case is not interchangeable with a known-eligible paper whose reporting fields are UNKNOWN; uncertain membership prevents treating the denominator as settled.
4. **Method coding complete:** only after that freeze, inspect the article and available methods/SI for all four registered fields with row dates, primary-source locators and supporting evidence, then complete the independent pass. Missing methods/access becomes UNKNOWN, never No. A known Yes for any of the three primary checks rules out “none of three” even if another field is unknown.
5. **P-LIT verdict available:** use all included papers as N, the registered lower bound `known_none/N` and upper bound `(known_none + potentially_none_unknown)/N`. HELD requires lower bound strictly greater than 0.80; FALSIFIED requires upper bound at most 0.80; otherwise INCOMPLETE EVIDENCE. Unknown method fields can remain in a completed accounting with bounds; unresolved discovery or membership cannot be converted into those bounds without a different registered model. The fourth deposition field remains descriptive.

The defensible final coverage phrase is completion of the **amended registered search over its stated window**, with one database plus its named complementary sources and documented limitations. It is not exhaustive dual-database discovery or proof that every eligible paper in existence was found. Until these boundaries are met, report the achieved retrieval/screening/reference counts and the unresolved coverage reason separately. A calendar deadline does not convert an incomplete audit into a positive result.
