## 1. WHY THIS ARM EXISTS

A7.1 / P-PROJ is CONFIRMED on one metal. Cr, U = 7.15 eV, 1×1, `HUBBARD (atomic)` against
`(ortho-atomic)` at byte-identical geometry: η 1.155 V vs 1.642 V, Δ = 0.487 V, and the
potential-limiting step flips (pls 2 → 1). Registered blind with a 0.10 V trigger; fired at ~5×.

**The standing objection is n = 1**, and it is a good objection: Cr's η(U) curve is a V with its
minimum at U = 3.5, so Cr is unusually sensitive in exactly that region. Nothing yet distinguishes
"the projector moves the answer" from "CrO₂ near its apex is fragile."

This arm answers that, and only that.

## 3. THE PRIMARY STATISTIC — |Δη|, and why not the residual

**Primary, registered:** `Δη_M = η_M(ortho) − η_M(atomic)`, in volts, at fixed geometry and
U = 7.50, per metal.

This is A7.1's own estimand. It needs no new threshold, no borrowed transfer, and it is the
quantity the headline claims.

**Rejected, and the rejection is recorded because the first draft of this arm proposed it.** An
earlier version proposed registering `R_M`, the RMS residual of a one-parameter U-shift fit, on the
argument that η = max(ladder step) − 1.23 makes an eV residual and a volt commensurate. **The
identity is real; the inference is false.** A uniform offset `c` applied to all three cumulative ΔG
moves step 1 by +c, step 4 by −c, and steps 2 and 3 by zero — so at pls 2 or 3:

| uniform offset | R | measured Δη |
|---|---|---|
| 0.05 eV | 0.0500 eV | **−0.0000 V** |
| 0.40 eV | 0.4000 eV | **+0.0000 V** |

A CONFIRM criterion on `R` can fire on a difference that moves the reported observable by nothing.
Across the six metals a fixed `R = 0.10 eV` corresponds to |Δη| from **0.0028 V (Fe) to 0.1277 V
(Ti) — a 46× spread.** And under the grid-decimation null the legs agree to 0.0035 eV while η
disagrees by 0.155 V.

**`R_M` is retained as a named DIAGNOSTIC for U-irreducibility only**, reported per metal with no
verdict attached. If it is ever given a threshold, that threshold derives from its own null — the
0.0035 eV interpolation floor — and never from A7.1's volts.

**Secondary, reported without a threshold:** whether the potential-limiting step differs between
legs, per metal. This is the mechanism-level observable and no error bar absorbs it. It is counted
and reported, not scored.

## 4. THE DENOMINATOR IS FIVE

**Cr is calibration, not a scored member.** `|Δη(Cr)| = 0.487 V` at U = 7.15 is already known and
published inside this project; a U = 7.50 Cr point is a consistency check on the instrument, not
evidence. Cr is reported in every table, always labelled **CALIBRATION (post-hoc)**, and **is
excluded from every count.**

**The blind five are Mn, Fe, Ti, Ru, Ir.**

This is written explicitly because the failure mode is familiar: A7.2 confirmed 5 of 6 but on
exactly three robust members against a registered ≥3, zero margin; A7.3 landed NOT MET at 3 of 6 in
a band the registration never defined. Counting Cr here would mean only three of five blind metals
need fire to declare a class effect. **It is not counted.**

## 5. THRESHOLDS — every band defined in advance, including the middle

Per metal, on the blind five: **metal M FIRES if |Δη_M| > 0.10 V** (A7.1's own trigger, inherited
unchanged). **M is NULL if |Δη_M| < 0.03 V** (A7.1's own falsification floor). Between 0.03 and
0.10 V, M is **INTERMEDIATE** and is reported as such — never rounded to either side.

Class verdict, on the count of FIRES out of five:

| fires | verdict | the sentence that may be written |
|---|---|---|
| **5 or 4** | **CONFIRMED** | the projector split is a property of the method across this roster, not of CrO₂ |
| **3 or 2** | **MIDDLE BAND — metal-dependent** | the split is real on some systems and not others; it is not universal, and the per-metal table is the result. **No class claim.** |
| **1** | **NOT MET** | one blind metal is an existence proof and nothing more |
| **0** | **FALSIFIED** | the projector is not a live variable at this U outside Cr; A7.1 stands as a single-system result and must be reported that way for the rest of the campaign |

**The middle band is named in advance and carries pre-written wording**, because A7.3 fell into a
band its registration never defined and the cost of that is still being paid.

**Anti-selection clause.** All five blind metals are reported in every table, in every draft, always,
with their Δη, their band, their pls comparison and their `R_M`. **No subset may be quoted alone**,
and no metal may be dropped for any reason discovered after the outcome is visible. Cr travels with
them, labelled.

## 6. THE PSEUDOPOTENTIAL CONFOUND — declared before the run

`docs/45:35` records pseudopotential family as **"NOT MEASURED — TRANSFERRED, no size available …
UNREGISTERED — no amendment carries a PP/basis arm."** The census across the roster is **ultrasoft
{Cr, Mn, Ti, Ir}, PAW {Fe}, norm-conserving {Ru}** — so across the blind five: **ultrasoft
{Mn, Ti, Ir}, PAW {Fe}, NC {Ru}.**

**Clause, registered in advance and with the same force as the spin clause:**

> If the set of FIRING blind metals is **exactly {Mn, Ti, Ir}** — every ultrasoft metal and no
> other — the result is **DECLARED CONFOUNDED with pseudopotential family** and may **not** be
> reported as a class claim about DFT+U. It is reported as an observation whose partition
> coincides exactly with a known unmeasured confound, in the same sentence, always.
>
> The symmetric case — firing set exactly **{Fe, Ru}** — carries the same declaration.

Without this clause a 3-of-5 result on the ultrasoft metals would reproduce the A7.3 failure
structurally: a verdict perfectly aligned with an unmeasured partition.

**Also declared, not scored:** the k-mesh is not uniform across the roster — Cr and Mn slabs are
`9 4 1`, Fe/Ti/Ru/Ir are `8 4 1`. Harmless *within* a pair, since Δη is a paired difference at
byte-identical settings, but any cross-metal table crosses two meshes and must say so on its face.

**Spin:** the roster splits nspin=2 {Cr, Mn, Fe} against nspin=1 {Ti, Ru, Ir}. On the blind five
that is {Mn, Fe} against {Ti, Ru, Ir}. If the firing set is exactly one of those two, the same
DECLARED CONFOUNDED language applies. *(Note: a spin rider of the form "the firing set must contain
at least one of each" would be vacuous at n ≥ 4 on a 2+3 partition and is deliberately not used.)*

## 7. WHAT THIS ARM CANNOT SHOW — stated now, not when a referee says it

1. **It does not say which projector is right.** It measures how much the answer moves when a
   documented convention changes. It does not adjudicate the convention. This clause is load-bearing
   and travels with every statement of the result.
2. **Both legs are single points on geometries relaxed under the atomic projector.** Residual forces
   are unequal between legs. Whether the split survives relaxation is **not tested here** and must
   not be implied.
3. **One U.** U = 7.50 only. The U-dependence of the split is not mapped by this arm.
4. **No materials claim.** A7.5 forbids quoting an absolute η for Cr, Fe, Co or Ni as a materials
   claim, and every quantity here is a within-metal paired difference by construction.
5. **Coverage** is not varied unless the A3 rider runs separately.

## 8. THE DIAGNOSTIC THAT IS ALREADY BANKED (reported, not scored)

Fitting the Cr ortho leg at U = 7.15 onto Cr's own banked atomic ΔG(U) curves with a single free U
shift over the registered 0-9 eV grid leaves **RMS 0.4982 eV with the optimum pinned at the grid
edge**, and the implied U displacement is **not single-valued**: ΔG_OH demands +11.32 eV, ΔG_O
+1.04 eV, ΔG_OOH +0.27 eV, η +2.14 eV — a **42× spread across four observables from one
calculation**.

This is what refutes "the projector is just a reparameterisation of U." It is **zero-compute, from
data already seen, and therefore calibration** — it is reported with that label and scores nothing.
The same fit is run on the blind five when their outputs land, and *those* are blind.
