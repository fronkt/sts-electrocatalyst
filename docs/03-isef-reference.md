# 03 — ISEF Reference (secondary)

STS is the primary target, but ISEF patterns are a useful cross-check (and an
option if a regional-fair route is ever pursued). Key differences: ISEF is
reached via affiliated regional/state fairs, allows teams (≤3), and **rewards
fabricated-and-measured devices more strongly** than STS, which tolerates deep
computational/theoretical work better.

## Where a materials project belongs (ISEF 2026, 22 categories)

| Category | Scope / fit |
|---|---|
| **Materials Science (MATS)** | Best home for a heat-dissipation / TIM material **and** materials-ML. Subcategory **Computation & Theory (COM)** explicitly covers ML/modeling for materials design. |
| **Energy: Sustainable Materials & Design (EGSD)** | Only if framed as energy efficiency/generation/storage (the "Thermal" subcat is power *generation*, not cooling). A heat sink fits here only if pitched as solar/EV/GPU efficiency. |
| Physics & Astronomy → Condensed Matter (MAT) | Basic-science phonon/thermal-transport physics. |
| Chemistry → Materials Chemistry (MAT) | Synthesis-heavy materials chemistry. |
| Robotics / Software / Math | Where **pure-algorithm/ML** projects land — and tend to win **Special** (not category Grand) awards. |

## Winning examples (2024–2025)

- **Grace Sun (MATS 1st, 2024)** — "Novel Chemical Doping Strategy to Enhance N-Type Organic Electrochemical Transistors" — *organic electronics* (also $75k Top Innovator).
- **Siyaa Poddar (MATS 1st, 2025)** — colorimetric MOF for airborne silica/uranium detection — *MOFs/sensing*.
- **Oliver Wang (EGSD 1st, 2025)** — "A Hybrid Hydrogel-Heat Sink for Enhancing GPU Performance, Solar Panel Efficiency, and Electric Vehicle Range" — **the clearest recent thermal-management win**; won by framing the heat sink as quantified GPU/solar/EV energy gains.
- **Will Bao (MATS 3rd, 2024)** — "Machine-Learning-Driven Discovery of Novel Acoustic Metamaterials" — *ML + tangible materials outcome*.
- **Ryan Rezaei (ROBO, 2025)** — symmetry-preserving VAE + rectified-flow diffusion for materials discovery — **generative ML, but entered in Robotics → won a $5k Special Award, not a Grand Award** (illustrates the routing penalty for ML-only).

## Judging weights (Grand Award, 100 pts)

Research Question 10 · Design & Methodology 15 · **Execution 20** · **Creativity &
Potential Impact 20** · **Presentation/interview 35**.

→ The **interview is 35%**; judges probe understanding and independence. Winners
**build and measure something** with quantified deltas, lead with a sharp novelty
claim, and name a real-world application.

## Takeaways (transfer to STS)

1. Thermal **can** win — but framed around quantified real-world deltas (à la
   Oliver Wang), not abstract heat physics.
2. **Use ML as the engine, not the whole project**; close the loop with a
   fabricated, measured artifact.
3. Lead with novelty + a named application; over-prepare the interview /
   independence story.
