## Post-hoc interpretation (not pre-stated in docs/91; decides nothing)

**post-hoc, the eleven non-bonded sites.** After H removal each of these fragments stays an O2-like unit — O–O 1.2214489442075662–1.2315871549620336 A against the model's own free O2 at 1.2355966878690232 A (from 0.014 A shorter to 0.004 A shorter; no superoxo lengthening) — held 2.3851029331455087–2.9387735480780557 A from its nearest metal (weak tier; Cr33Co5Ni29Cu33 1/3 desorbs to 3.262281219145177 A) and 2.6724160839896185–2.9676809158923616 A from the nearest lattice O: a physisorption-like geometry; the same potential-energy surface, referenced to its own O2 molecule in the same cell, assigns it a binding of 0.4597098319308781–0.7147646829942271 eV before the fragment (ZPE − TS) (0.4097098319308781–0.6647646829942271 eV at 0.05 eV; 0.35970983193087813–0.6147646829942272 at 0.10). Read against the expectation that a PBE(+U)-trained PES with no dispersion term holds an unperturbed O2 several tenths of an A outside bonding distance by at most a few tenths of an eV — an expectation from outside the tree, not a measured anchor — this is a physically implausible pairing, and the reading here is that the surface-side energy is a model artefact; it is a reading, not a finding of internal inconsistency, since a single PES is free to carry such a minimum. **Direction of a reference error.** dG_ads = E(*O2) − E_slab − E_O2(model) + 0.05, so an error δ in the model's O2 molecule enters as −δ: an O2 the model overbinds (E_O2 too low, the raw-PBE sign) pushes dG_ads positive, not negative, and for a fragment whose O–O sits at the free-molecule value the molecule-internal error is shared by E(*O2) and E_O2 and cancels; the "spurious underbinding of O on U-corrected metals" that `docs/91:24` quotes from Warford, Thiemann and Csanyi (arXiv:2601.21056) likewise raises dG_ads. The residual attraction on these eleven is therefore a slab–fragment interaction term of the PES and not a gas-reference offset. The selective Hubbard-U training mixture of `docs/91:24` (with Karimitari et al., arXiv:2605.09394, OOH* under-represented) can still be its origin — a mixture-trained PES treating an O2 unit near an oxide surface differently from the same unit in vacuum — but that is a surface-side error and its sign is not the sign a free-O2 error carries; the `docs/91:63` reason for booking the bridge fourth step as 4.92 − dG(*O2 + H_b) rather than through a bare *O2 (the reference chain would re-import the model's O2-molecule error) stands on its own and is not what this diagnostic tests. **The clean-slab alternative.** E_slab is the clean relaxed slab of the decoration (equal on all 13, above), and the fragment-state slab sits 0.04915532509883216–0.35522270712924675 A (free-atom max) from it on twelve sites and 0.5766915021549353 A on Cr33Co5Ni29Cu33 1/3; if the OOH-history slab occupies a lower basin than the clean relaxation found (`docs/91:85`, risk 7), part of E(*O2) − E_slab is slab relaxation and not fragment binding, with no O2-molecule error at all. No O2-stripped single point of a fragment slab exists in the tree to separate the two (it would need a model load; none is licensed), so this is not excluded; what bounds it is the size of the displacement against the energy — Mn31Ni31Co33Cu6 1/3 at 0.04915532509883216 A carries −0.6320625819478476 eV (0.00 column), Cu8Cr23Mn35Co34 2/0 at 0.06109021311205829 A carries −0.5603430071613626 eV, Ni34Fe6Cu29Co31 2/3 at 0.06612078938260493 A carries −0.6648257205044388 eV — a reading, not a measurement.
