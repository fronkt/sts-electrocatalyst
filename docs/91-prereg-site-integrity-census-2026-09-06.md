**(c) DESCRIPTOR-DEFINED RANKING of the six gated compositions under four rules.** The ranking site set is the twelve CENSUS-1 sites per composition (seeds 0, 1, 2 x site_index 0..3) and nothing else; every rule is a minimum over its qualifying sites, an extreme-value statistic that decreases with the number of sites drawn and is comparable only at equal site count (the 120-site distribution of CENSUS-3 is readout (f) and never enters a rule). The rules: banked (min over all twelve, the screen's own aggregation, unconverged sites included as the box included them); intact-only (min over INTACT sites); adsorbate-intact-only (min over ADSORBATE-INTACT sites); two-pathway (min over sites whose OOH endpoint is *OOH — the cus pathway — or a bound-or-weak *O2 + H_b — the bridge pathway; sites whose endpoint is desorbed, H-free or O-O-cleaved are excluded as pathway-undefined, since their retained third-state energy is that of a product, not of an intermediate). The pathway bookkeeping is the same four-step CHE under both labels: steps [dG_1, dG_O − dG_1, dG_3 − dG_O, 4.92 − dG_3], eta = max step − 1.23 V, where dG_3 is the retained third-state free energy — E(*OOH) or E(*O2 + H_b) as the geometry says, with the screen's OOH correction — and the fourth step of the bridge pathway is *O2 + H_b -> O2(g) + * + H+ + e−, booked as 4.92 − dG(*O2 + H_b) exactly as in Svane and Rossmeisl 2022, with no *O2 intermediate: referencing a bare *O2 fragment through the H2O/H2 cycle would re-import the model's O2-molecule error that the 4.92 eV convention removes (src/hea_oer/referencing.py:4-8), and a desorbed fragment would collapse that energy onto the model's own 2H2O -> O2 + 2H2 value. ## 3. Isolation

Everything lands under results/site_census_2026-09-06/ (manifests/, results/, logs/, readout/, torch-cache/, status.json); nothing is written under results/cr_site_chains_2026-09-06/, results/site_diagnostic_2026-09-06/, results/ranking_adequacy_2026-09-06/, runs/ or anvil/. The source screens are read from the tracked LF copies and never rewritten. The driver's own guards enforce the rest: a manifest whose model bytes or implementation hashes drift is refused; an output that exists is refused without `--resume`; `--resume` with a different thread count or package set is refused (screen_diagnostic.py:211-212). The runner (src/scripts/site_census_runner.py) launches `screen_diagnostic.py run` as hidden subprocesses (CREATE_NO_WINDOW), `--threads 2` always (a different value is refused at the command line), up to four at once (a worker count with workers x 2 > cpu_count is refused, not capped), in the queue order of §1, with the environment inherited plus TORCHINDUCTOR_CACHE_DIR = results/site_census_2026-09-06/torch-cache and PYTHONUNBUFFERED = 1, with stdout and stderr appended to logs/<manifest>.log, a status JSON refreshed at least every 60 s, `--resume` on every restart, removal of a stale `.lock` only when the pid it records is dead, and a stop file (results/site_census_2026-09-06/STOP: no new launches; containing KILL: running processes terminated). The readout (src/scripts/site_census_readout.py) refuses to run while any manifest's result is missing or unfinished unless `--partial`, and then lists the missing manifests in every output file.

Launch, from the repository root: `python src/scripts/site_census_runner.py --dry-run` prints the 103-line plan; `python src/scripts/site_census_runner.py --start` runs it in the foreground; `--detach` spawns the same runner hidden. Readout: `python src/scripts/site_census_readout.py [--partial] [--o2-records <1b records>]` -> results/site_census_2026-09-06/readout/{per_site.csv, per_site.json, reproduction.json, ranking.json, distribution.json}.

## 4. Named risks

1. CPU contention. Other relaxation processes may run on this machine at the same time; four 2-thread census workers plus that load exceed 8 cores, and wall time — not any number — is what suffers. The realised figure is reported with the contention noted.
2. Weight identity. mace 0.3.15 on CPU against the box's 0.3.16 on a V100; a NOT REPRODUCED composition under (a) is reported as such and the identity question stays open. The three other checkpoints carry no historical claim at all.
3. Thread count. `--resume` refuses a checkpoint whose recorded environment differs; every census process must run at `--threads 2`, and the runner enforces it. A process launched by hand at another thread count creates a result that cannot be resumed.
4. Site enumeration. The four sites are the first four cus sites by `cus_site_xy` enumeration on the pristine slab, not a random sample of the surface; in the retained chains site 0 is cation index 16. Seeds 3..29 sample decorations, not sites within a decoration. No statement about the surface population follows from site fractions.
5. Kill granularity. The driver checkpoints per candidate; a kill loses the unfinished candidate. CENSUS-1 candidates are ~1.7-2.9 h; CENSUS-3 blocks are sized to the same.
6. The `historical_label` field of every manifest reads `medium-mpa-0` regardless of checkpoint (driver behaviour); the checkpoint is identified by filename and bytes hash only.
7. BFGS cap non-convergence. With up to 11,079 adsorbate relaxations at a 300-step cap, and no relax set in this project having completed without retries (docs/76:272-275), unconverged states are expected. They are classified like every other state, are never INTACT, are counted per composition and per model, and stay inside the banked rule exactly as the box kept them; a composition whose banked minimum sits on an unconverged site is reported with that fact beside the number.
8. Ensemble checkpoint cost. The CENSUS-2 checkpoints have no measured per-step cost (the 2-step smokes of (e) measure overhead only); the queue runs their gated six before their other six so that the first realised manifest per checkpoint is available before the bulk of that checkpoint's cost is spent.

## 5. What this does NOT do

No DFT is run or licensed. No line of docs/43 is added, no THRESHOLD is registered, no prediction is scored, no S8 or melt decision is taken, results/r4_melt_list.json is not regenerated or changed, and nothing enters the body-figure ledger (cap reached, docs/43:1930). No MLIP is fine-tuned and no external corpus is scanned (docs/43:1943, :1957). No UMA leg and no MACE-MH-1 leg runs. Nothing here validates, ranks or certifies an electrode; the rutile slabs are imposed models (docs/cr-site-chain-readout-2026-09-06.md:44). A CENSUS-1b module, a UMA driver, an MH-1 leg and any DFT panel on census geometries each need their own dated line before they run.
